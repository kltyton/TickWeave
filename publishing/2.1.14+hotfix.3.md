# TickWeave 2.1.14+hotfix.3

## 中文

修复并行区块与方块实体操作中的方块实体集合及 Forge onLoad 队列交接竞争，保留原生加载回调。
修复 Forge 动态光照回调读取方块实体时等待完整区块、阻塞光照和区块加载的问题。
修复未被 Mixin 处理的模组实体缺失字段布局、遇到客户端字段类型后无法捕获共享状态的问题，涵盖 Small Ships 的服务端兼容问题。
保留实体、玩家连接、刷怪和区块工作的并行主链。

## English

Fixes concurrent block-entity collections and Forge onLoad handoff while preserving native load callbacks.
Keeps Forge dynamic-light block-entity queries within available lighting chunks instead of requesting FULL chunks.
Reads field layouts for mod entity classes without Mixin targets, including Small Ships classes with unavailable client field types on dedicated servers.
Retains parallel entity, player connection, spawning, and chunk processing.
