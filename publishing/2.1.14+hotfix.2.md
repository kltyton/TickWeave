# TickWeave 2.1.14+hotfix.2

## 中文

修复共享标量延迟表的并发读写与遍历竞争，包含 FTB Quests 背包检测队列；保留原生期限、首次排队和回调行为。修复 Forge 多部件实体登记表在碰撞、推挤和刷怪查询中出现迭代器异常或空部件。保留上一版 Rhino 死锁修复，实体 tick、玩家连接和区块刷怪继续并行。

## English

Fixes concurrent mutation and traversal of analyzed scalar delay tables, including FTB Quests inventory detection, while retaining native deadlines, first-insertion behavior and callbacks. Fixes iterator failures and null parts in Forge multipart entity queries used by collisions, pushing and spawning. Retains the previous Rhino deadlock fix; entity ticking, player connections and chunk spawning remain parallel.
