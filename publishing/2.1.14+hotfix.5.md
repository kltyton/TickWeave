## English

- Keep Forge item pickup events on the server thread while retaining ownership of the item and player. This fixes live storage access failures during backpack magnet pickups and preserves event cancellation and pickup results.
- Commit player section, distance tickets and player-map registration together. Movement and removal cannot observe a partially updated registration; entity tracking and chunk delivery remain on their existing parallel paths.
- Claim entity removal before running its callbacks, preventing duplicate or reentrant removal from repeating tracking cleanup.
- Includes the shared record-key cache and inventory callback fixes from hotfix.4.

## 中文

- Forge 物品拾取事件在服务器主线程执行，并保留物品与玩家归属。修复背包磁铁拾取时访问实时存储引发的异常，保留事件取消和拾取结果。
- 玩家区段、距离票据和玩家表登记在同一事务内提交，移动与移除不会读到半完成的登记；实体追踪和区块发送保留原有并行路径。
- 实体移除在执行回调前取得唯一执行资格，避免重复或重入移除再次清理追踪登记。
- 包含 hotfix.4 的 record 键共享缓存及物品回调修复。
