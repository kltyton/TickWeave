# TickWeave 2.1.17

## 中文

完善玩家区块登记兼容，支持带有条件分支和异常清理的登记流程，保留跟踪与卸载回调的执行顺序。

启用 Radium 共装时已有的并发活动导航登记与方块更新监听处理。实体、玩家连接及区块工作继续并行。

将玩家区块发送回调与卸载操作纳入完整的主线程事务，保持发送状态、队列顺序和限额一致。

适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1、Java 17 的 Forge 和 Fabric。

## English

Improves player chunk-registration compatibility with conditional branches and exception cleanup, preserving the order of tracking and unload callbacks.

Enables concurrent active-navigation registration and block-update listener handling when Radium is installed. Entity ticking, player connections and chunk work remain parallel.

Coordinates player chunk-delivery callbacks and unload operations as complete main-thread transactions, preserving send state, queue order and limits.

For Minecraft 1.20.1 on Java 17, with Forge and Fabric builds.
